# Screening — Funniest Lost Sock Status Update

Mission: `u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-lost-sock-status-update-4b1fa3ce`
Judge criteria, in order (RULES.txt):
1. Is a status update in the voice of a lost sock, and the funniest such; anything that is not a status update from a lost sock loses to any that is.
2. Earns a real laugh, with a turn, surprise or punchline landing in the last few words.
3. Rests on a clear comic premise the sock's situation makes inevitable; the joke feels found, not assembled.
4. Sounds like a sock: small, domestic, a little anxious about the dryer, never like a person wearing a sock as a costume.
5. Feels new, and twists the obvious sock material (the dryer, the missing partner) somewhere unexpected.

Banned/stock angles (used none, except where twisted): the sock is merely "in the dryer"; the sock merely misses its partner with no twist; generic "where did my sock go" with no location and no punch.

Scoring: 1–5 per criterion, in order. Criterion 1 is a hard gate: any candidate scoring below 5 on it is out.
